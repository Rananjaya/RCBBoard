RCBBoard is a keyboard for English and Sinhala that works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what RCBBoard does with the text you type and the other information it handles.

## Summary

- RCBBoard **has no internet access**. It does not have Android's `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot send what you type, or anything else, over the network.
- RCBBoard has **no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts** and no third-party services.
- The developer **does not collect, receive or sell any of your data**. Everything RCBBoard stores stays in the app's storage on your phone, unless you or your phone's backup copy it elsewhere (see "Backups and sharing").

## What RCBBoard handles, and why

### The text you type
RCBBoard needs to see what you type in order to work: to show the keys you press in the app you are using, suggest words, correct typos and switch languages. This happens on your phone only. Typed text is never sent anywhere. RCBBoard does not write the text you type into its logs.

Like every Android keyboard, RCBBoard can see what you type in any app while it is your keyboard. Android shows a warning about this when you turn a keyboard on.

### Learning from your typing (personalised suggestions)
To improve suggestions, RCBBoard remembers words you use and which words you often type after each other. This is turned on by default and can be turned off in **Settings → Text correction → Personalized suggestions**; turning it off deletes what was learned.

- Learned words are stored only in the app's storage on your phone.
- A word is saved about 25 seconds after you type it, so a typo you correct straight away is not learned.
- RCBBoard does **not** learn from **password fields**, in **incognito mode**, or in fields where the app you type in asks keyboards not to learn (for example some name fields).
- Words you remove from suggestions ("blocked" words) are stored on your phone so they are not suggested again.

### Personal dictionary (optional, off by default)
If you turn on **Add words to the personal dictionary**, words you type that are not in RCBBoard's dictionary can be added to Android's personal dictionary, a feature of your phone that other keyboards and apps may also use. You can edit or delete these words in your phone's language settings.

### Dictionaries
RCBBoard includes its English and Sinhala dictionaries in the app. It does not download dictionaries, and the standard app does not let you add dictionary files.

### Clipboard history
RCBBoard keeps a history of text and images you copy so you can paste them again. It is turned on by default and can be turned off in **Settings → Preferences**.

- The history is stored only on your phone. Unpinned items are deleted automatically after the retention time (10 minutes by default, adjustable); pinned items stay until you remove them.
- Items that the app you copied from marks as **sensitive** (for example passwords or one-time codes from password managers, on Android 13 and newer) are **not** saved in the history.
- Incognito mode does not stop the clipboard history; turn the history off if you do not want it.
- You can delete the whole history at any time from the clipboard view.

### Recently used emoji
RCBBoard remembers the emoji you use recently (from the emoji keyboard, emoji search, emoji suggestions and the optional emoji row) to show them first. They are stored only on your phone, are not remembered in incognito mode, and can be cleared in the emoji keyboard.

### Contact names (optional, off by default)
If you turn on **Settings → Text correction → Look up contact names**, RCBBoard asks for permission to read your contacts and uses the names to suggest and correct them as you type. The names are read on your phone and kept in a local dictionary file in the app's storage. They are never sent anywhere. Turn the setting off, or remove the Contacts permission in Android's settings, to stop this.

### App names (optional, off by default)
If you turn on **Look up app names**, RCBBoard uses the names of apps installed on your phone for suggestions. This happens only on your phone.

### Incognito mode
With **Force incognito mode** turned on (Settings → Advanced), or when an app asks for it, RCBBoard does not learn new words and does not remember used emoji. Clipboard history is not affected.

### Logs and crash reports
RCBBoard keeps a short technical log in memory and may save a crash report on your phone if it crashes. These never contain the text you type. They are only shared if you choose to: *Settings → About → Save log* saves the log to a file you pick, and RCBBoard may offer to save a crash report file. Nothing is sent automatically.

## Where your data is stored

All of the above is stored in RCBBoard's private storage on your phone, protected by Android's app sandbox. Other apps cannot read it. Some of it is kept in storage that Android makes available before you unlock the phone after a restart, so that the keyboard works on the lock screen.

## Backups and sharing

- **Your own backup file:** *Settings → Advanced → Backup and restore* creates a backup file at a location you choose. It contains your settings, learned words, blocked words, clipboard history, custom layouts and images or fonts you added. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Restoring it brings this data back.
- **Android's device backup:** if you have turned on backup in your phone's settings (for example Google backup), Android may include RCBBoard's data in your phone's backup, stored in your own account with your backup provider. RCBBoard and its developer have no access to it. You control this in your phone's backup settings.
- **Links:** some entries in the settings (for example *Source code* and *Privacy policy*) open a web page in your browser. RCBBoard itself does not connect to the internet.

## Data retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall RCBBoard. You can:
- turn off personalized suggestions to delete learned words;
- clear the clipboard history and recently used emoji;
- turn off contact names and app names;
- delete all RCBBoard data with **Android Settings → Apps → RCBBoard → Storage → Clear storage**, or by uninstalling the app.

Because the developer does not receive any of your data, there is no data held by the developer to delete.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Read and write personal dictionary | To show and, if you turn it on, add words to Android's personal dictionary |
| Contacts (only if you turn on contact names) | To suggest names from your contacts |
| Vibrate | Key press vibration |
| Run at startup | To apply the "show app icon" setting after the phone restarts (Android 9 and older) |

RCBBoard does not request internet, location, microphone, camera, phone or SMS permissions.

## Children

RCBBoard is a general keyboard app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

## Your rights

You have full control over the data RCBBoard stores, using the options above. Because no personal data is collected by or sent to the developer, there is no personal data held by the developer to access, correct or delete.
