RCBBoard is a free, open-source keyboard for English and Sinhala. It includes English (US and UK), Sinhala, Sinhala Wijesekara and Sinhala Singlish input.

Your typing stays on your device. RCBBoard has no internet permission, ads, analytics or tracking.

Features:
<ul>
  <li>English and Sinhala typing, suggestions and autocorrection</li>
  <li>Switch between enabled languages from the keyboard</li>
  <li>Optional emoji row with recently used emoji</li>
  <li>Clipboard history with controls to clear or disable it</li>
  <li>Personalized suggestions that learn on your device and can be turned off</li>
  <li>Customizable themes, colors, key layouts and toolbar</li>
  <li>One-handed mode, split keyboard and number pad</li>
  <li>First-run setup tutorial with a replay option in Settings</li>
  <li>Backup and restore for settings and personal data</li>
</ul>

Contact suggestions are optional and require permission.
